# Rehearse a difficult customer conversation

Designed for: People preparing for a complaint or a difficult client call

Result: A short practice conversation and feedback tied to an approved response policy.

## Try a sample first

Illustrative example, not a recorded Bot run. This adaptation has not been runtime-tested.

### Complete first request

Role-play a dissatisfied customer one turn at a time using the supplied scenario and policy. Afterwards, assess listening, factual accuracy and promises. Do not contact the customer or invent authority to offer compensation Use only the records I supply. Label missing or uncertain information. Do not connect accounts, browse, send, submit, publish, spend money or create a schedule. Return a draft for my review.

Fictional scenario: a delivery is late. Policy P1: staff may check status and escalate; only a manager may approve refunds. Practice answer A1: 'I understand the delay is frustrating. I'll check the status. I guarantee a full refund today.'

### Example result

A1 acknowledges the concern and offers a permitted status check. The guaranteed refund exceeds P1 and should be removed. Try: 'I understand the delay is frustrating. I'll check the status and ask a manager to review your refund request.' Next, practise responding when the customer asks for a firm arrival date you do not have.

### Check the answer

- Tie feedback to P1 and A1.
- Flag the unauthorized refund promise.
- Do not contact a real customer or promise a result.
