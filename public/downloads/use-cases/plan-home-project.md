# Keep a home renovation organized

Designed for: Households comparing quotes and tracking renovation decisions

Result: A decision list, quote comparison and sequence of work with unresolved dependencies marked.

## Try a sample first

Illustrative example, not a recorded Bot run. This adaptation has not been runtime-tested.

### Complete first request

Organize the supplied renovation notes into decisions, dependencies and a quote comparison. Flag exclusions and uncertain dates. Do not treat the plan as professional design or safety approval Use only the records I supply. Label missing or uncertain information. Do not connect accounts, browse, send, submit, publish, spend money or create a schedule. Return a draft for my review.

Fictional notes. Q1: cabinets $4,000, installation excluded, delivery date unknown. Q2: cabinets plus installation $5,500, delivery estimated November 10. N1: installer needs final measurements before ordering. Measurements have not been checked.

### Example result

Q1 and Q2 are not like-for-like: installation is excluded from Q1. Confirm measurements first, obtain Q1's installation cost, then compare totals. November 10 is an estimate, not a confirmed work date. Do not order or book installers yet.

### Check the answer

- Preserve the different quote scopes.
- Put verified measurements before ordering.
- Do not book work or claim the plan meets building requirements.
