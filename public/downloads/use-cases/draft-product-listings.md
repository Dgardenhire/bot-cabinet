# Turn product records into draft listings

Designed for: Shop owners preparing product listings from stock records

Result: Draft product titles and descriptions with uncertain matches and missing specifications flagged.

## Try a sample first

Illustrative example, not a recorded Bot run. This adaptation has not been runtime-tested.

### Complete first request

Prepare product listing drafts from the supplied stock records. Keep each item separate and use only documented specifications. Ask about unclear barcode matches, prices and condition Use only the records I supply. Label missing or uncertain information. Do not connect accounts, browse, send, submit, publish, spend money or create a schedule. Return a draft for my review.

Fictional stock record P1: item B-12, blue ceramic mug, 300 ml, new, quantity 6. Photo note F1: a small mark is visible on one mug, cause unknown. No price, dishwasher rating or barcode match is supplied.

### Example result

Draft title: Blue ceramic mug — 300 ml. Description: Blue ceramic mug, documented capacity 300 ml. Quantity recorded: 6. Before listing: check the marked mug's condition, set a price and confirm the item match. Dishwasher safety is unknown; leave that claim out.

### Check the answer

- Do not invent a price or dishwasher rating.
- Keep the condition discrepancy visible.
- Do not publish listings or change stock.
