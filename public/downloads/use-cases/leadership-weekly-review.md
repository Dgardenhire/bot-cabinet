# Leadership weekly review

Designed for: Leaders and small teams that need a clear weekly view of priorities, capacity, and blocked decisions

Result: A current operating brief and a realistic proposed plan for the next week, with ideas kept separate from commitments.

## Bots

1. COACH
2. Chief of Staff
3. Planner

## Information to gather

- Confirmed priorities and commitments
- The week's notes, results, and decisions
- Next week's capacity, obligations, and deadlines

## Workflow

### 1. COACH

Guide a review of what changed, what remains open, and what may need to stop

Output: A decision frame separating ideas, plans, and commitments

Message to send:

Use the approved inputs I provide. Guide a review of what changed, what remains open, and what may need to stop. Return this result: A decision frame separating ideas, plans, and commitments. Ask me about missing information before you continue.

### 2. Chief of Staff

Compare the review with confirmed priorities, owners, dependencies, and capacity

Output: A priority and decision log with conflicts and missing owners marked

Message to send:

Start with this result from COACH: A decision frame separating ideas, plans, and commitments. Compare the review with confirmed priorities, owners, dependencies, and capacity. Return this result: A priority and decision log with conflicts and missing owners marked. Ask me about missing information before you continue.

### 3. Planner

Turn only the approved priorities into a proposed sequence for the next week

Output: A weekly plan with owners, dependencies, and checkpoints

Message to send:

Start with this result from Chief of Staff: A priority and decision log with conflicts and missing owners marked. Turn only the approved priorities into a proposed sequence for the next week. Return this result: A weekly plan with owners, dependencies, and checkpoints. Ask me about missing information before you continue.

## Overall request

Run a weekly leadership review from these approved notes. Keep ideas separate from commitments, surface priority and capacity conflicts, and propose next week's plan without assigning work or changing shared records.

## Decisions for a person

- Decide what remains an idea and what becomes a priority
- Confirm assignments, deadlines, and capacity
- Approve any changes to calendars, task systems, or team messages

## First test

Review one week from copied notes and compare every listed commitment with the source before changing any shared system.
