# New venture evaluation

Designed for: Founders and small teams deciding whether an idea deserves a real-world test

Result: An evidence-based venture brief and a small test with success, revise, and stop rules set in advance.

## Bots

1. NOVA
2. Researcher
3. PULSE

## Information to gather

- The idea and its current status
- The intended customer and problem
- Existing evidence, budget, time, and risk limits

## Workflow

### 1. NOVA

Separate known facts, assumptions, forecasts, and missing evidence

Output: A venture brief and assumption ledger

Message to send:

Use the approved inputs I provide. Separate known facts, assumptions, forecasts, and missing evidence. Return this result: A venture brief and assumption ledger. Ask me about missing information before you continue.

### 2. Researcher

Check the highest-risk assumptions against approved public sources

Output: A source-based evidence brief with conflicts and gaps

Message to send:

Start with this result from NOVA: A venture brief and assumption ledger. Check the highest-risk assumptions against approved public sources. Return this result: A source-based evidence brief with conflicts and gaps. Ask me about missing information before you continue.

### 3. PULSE

Turn the most important open question into one measurable low-cost test

Output: A test plan with a primary metric and success, revise, and stop rules

Message to send:

Start with this result from Researcher: A source-based evidence brief with conflicts and gaps. Turn the most important open question into one measurable low-cost test. Return this result: A test plan with a primary metric and success, revise, and stop rules. Ask me about missing information before you continue.

## Overall request

Evaluate this idea without treating it as a commitment. Separate evidence from assumptions, check the most important unknowns, and propose one low-cost test with decision rules for my approval.

## Decisions for a person

- Confirm the idea's status and risk limits
- Approve any spending, outreach, or public claim
- Decide whether to continue, revise, or stop after reviewing the results

## First test

Use one idea and approved public sources to design a no-spend test. Approve the decision rules before running it.
