# Prepare a warranty claim

Designed for: People gathering receipts and warranty terms for a faulty product

Result: A claim draft with the purchase details, supporting records and missing information clearly marked.

## Try a sample first

Illustrative example, not a recorded Bot run. This adaptation has not been runtime-tested.

### Complete first request

Organize the purchase record and supplied warranty terms, identify missing claim details, and draft a factual warranty request. Do not promise eligibility or a refund Use only the records I supply. Label missing or uncertain information. Do not connect accounts, browse, send, submit, publish, spend money or create a schedule. Return a draft for my review.

Fictional records. R1: Alder desk lamp, $80, purchased September 1, 2026. W1: one-year warranty for manufacturing defects; proof of purchase required. N1: lamp stopped working October 10; cause unknown. No serial number or inspection is supplied.

### Example result

The receipt records the purchase within W1's stated one-year period, but the cause is unknown, so coverage is not established. Draft: 'Please review this warranty request for my Alder desk lamp. It stopped working October 10. I can provide R1. Please confirm the claim steps and any serial number or inspection you need.' Not submitted.

### Check the answer

- Distinguish the warranty period from proof of coverage.
- Request missing details without inventing a defect.
- Do not submit a claim or arrange a return.
