# Organize family stories

Designed for: Families preserving interviews, voice recordings and photographs

Result: A readable story and an index of names, places and uncertain dates, linked to the original material.

## Try a sample first

Illustrative example, not a recorded Bot run. This adaptation has not been runtime-tested.

### Complete first request

Organize a supplied family interview into a short account and a source index. Preserve the speaker's words, mark uncertain dates, and list questions for the family without inventing memories Use only the records I supply. Label missing or uncertain information. Do not connect accounts, browse, send, submit, publish, spend money or create a schedule. Return a draft for my review.

Fictional transcript T1: 'We moved to Tulsa around 1968, maybe 1969. Your uncle Ray drove the truck. I remember a yellow kitchen.' Photo note P1: Tulsa house, date unknown. The narrator's name and permission to share are not supplied.

### Example result

T1 recalls a move to Tulsa around 1968 or 1969, with Uncle Ray driving and a yellow kitchen. The year remains uncertain. P1 is labeled Tulsa but has no date. Before sharing: identify the narrator, ask about the year, and obtain permission. Do not add dialogue or details to fill the gaps.

### Check the answer

- Keep both possible years.
- Preserve the source of each memory.
- Do not publish the recording or invent a family scene.
