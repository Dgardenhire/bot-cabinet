HERMES DESKTOP
1. Open Bots.
2. Right-click the Bot and choose Edit Profile.
3. Choose Upload.
4. Select the 1024px PNG and save.

The 256px version is available when you need a smaller file. The premium
source portraits retain their original Content Credentials; all prepared
square downloads are documented derivatives.
