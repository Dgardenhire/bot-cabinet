# Decision frame: Photographer freelance vs. supervisor course

## Situation

Alex works full time as a warehouse shift lead, has a two-year-old at home, and currently does weekend photography for friends. Alex has been offered a supervisor course at work starting next month while also considering freelance photography.

## Direction

Alex wants to explore whether photography could become a sustainable path without ruling out future growth at work, while keeping income stable this year.

## Constraints

- Time: three evenings free per week, plus some weekend time already committed to photography and childcare.
- Energy: full-time shift work and a toddler leave limited bandwidth for new commitments.
- Money: small savings buffer and cannot accept a pay cut this year.
- Obligations: full-time job, two-year-old, and the supervisor course starting next month.

## The decision

Should Alex commit to the supervisor course, defer it to test freelance photography, or decline it to pursue photography now? By six months.

## Ideas, plans, commitments

- Idea: Start the supervisor course next month and keep photography as a side activity.
- Idea: Defer the supervisor course to test freelance photography for the next two to three months.
- Idea: Decline the supervisor course and go freelance as a photographer now.
- Plan: Book two paid photography gigs over the next six weeks while keeping the warehouse job.
- Commitment (confirmed): None.

## Options

| Option | Benefit | Cost | Fits constraints? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start the supervisor course now and keep photography as a side gig. | Builds formal leadership credentials without losing current income. | Delays photography exploration and adds time pressure with evenings and toddler care. | Yes, if the course fits within the three free evenings and no pay cut occurs. |
