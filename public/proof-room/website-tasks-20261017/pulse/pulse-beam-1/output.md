Observation
- 100 unique visitors; 10 clicked setup (10%); 2 recorded a first result (2% of visitors; 20% of setup clickers). No repeat-use event exists, so retention is unmeasured.

Baseline (primary metric)
- First-result rate = first results ÷ unique visitors = 2/100 = 2%. Secondary: setup click rate = 10%; setup-to-first-result conversion = 2/10 = 20%.

Interpretation (tentative, small sample)
- The bottleneck appears after setup click, not before (20% completion), but n=2 prevents any reliable inference. Traffic quality, setup friction, and instrumentation gaps are all plausible and indistinguishable from this data.

Missing evidence
- Repeat-use/retention metric and event definition.
- Where setup clickers drop off (steps, errors, time-to-first-result).
- Traffic source/segment and device.
- Whether "first result" is correctly instrumented and consistently defined.
- Sample size and any control/comparison group.

Proposed experiments (not yet launched; require approval before any change)

Experiment A — Reduce setup-to-first-result friction (guided first run / starter template)
- Primary metric: setup-to-first-result conversion rate.
- Success rule: ≥35% conversion with ≥50 setup clicks per arm (or a pre-agreed minimum detectable effect); stop rule: no lift after minimum sample, or any drop in first-result quality/completion, or harm to downstream metrics.

Experiment B — Clarify value proposition before setup click (landing copy/CTA test)
- Primary metric: first-result rate per unique visitor.
- Success rule: ≥4% (2x baseline) with ≥500 visitors per arm; stop rule: if setup click rate falls or first-result rate does not improve at minimum sample, or any privacy/brand concern arises.

Decision
- Do not change tracking, spend, or claims now. Treat current counts as directional only; collect missing evidence and run one experiment at a time with pre-set rules before any conclusion.
