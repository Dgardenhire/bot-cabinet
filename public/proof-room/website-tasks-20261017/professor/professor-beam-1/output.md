## Explanation: Percent means "per hundred"

A percent is a way of expressing a number as parts out of 100. "Percent" literally means "per hundred," so 1% is 1/100, 50% is 50/100, and 100% is the whole. To find a part from a percent and a whole, treat the percent as a fraction of 100 and multiply: part = percent × whole (with the percent written as a decimal or fraction, e.g., 20% = 0.20 = 20/100).

Worked example: 20% of 80. Write 20% as 0.20, then 0.20 × 80 = 16. So 20% of 80 is 16.

## Answers (keep separate)

1. 15 (0.25 × 60 = 15)
2. 15% (12 ÷ 80 × 100 = 15)
3. 10% ((55 − 50) ÷ 50 × 100 = 10)
4. 15% (30 ÷ 200 × 100 = 15)
5. 25% ((30 − 40) ÷ 40 × 100 = −25; magnitude 25% decrease)
