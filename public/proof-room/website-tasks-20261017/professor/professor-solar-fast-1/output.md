## Explanation: What a percent means

The course notes define percent as “per hundred.” So 50% means 50 out of 100, and 10% means 10 out of 100. That is why 50% of anything is half of it and 10% is one tenth: 50 out of 100 is half, and 10 out of 100 is one tenth.

A percent can be written as a fraction over 100. For example, 12% is 12/100, which is 0.12 as a decimal. To find a percent of a number, write the percent as a decimal and multiply by the number.

Worked example: To find 12% of 80, convert 12% to 0.12, then multiply: 0.12 × 80 = 9.6. So 12% of 80 is 9.6.

## Practice

1. What is 50% of 80?
2. What is 10% of 80?
3. What percent of 80 is 12?
4. Increase 80 by 10%.
5. A price goes from 80 to 96. What is the percentage increase?

## Answers (keep separate)

1. 40
2. 8
3. 15% (12 ÷ 80 × 100 = 15)
4. 88 (10% of 80 = 8; 80 + 8 = 88)
5. 20% ((96 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = 20)
