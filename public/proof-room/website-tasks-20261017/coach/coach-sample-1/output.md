# Decision frame: Photography freelance versus warehouse supervisor course

## Situation

Alex works full time as a warehouse shift lead, has three evenings a week free, a small savings buffer, and a two-year-old at home. Alex does weekend photography for friends and enjoys it.

## Direction

Alex wants to explore whether freelance photography can become a viable path while preserving financial stability and family time.

## Constraints

- Time: Full-time warehouse shift lead; three evenings free per week; parenting a two-year-old.
- Energy: Not currently measured; depends on child-care support and sleep.
- Money: Small savings buffer; no pay cut possible this year.
- Obligations: Full-time employment; primary caregiving for a two-year-old.

## The decision

Should Alex pursue freelance photography now or stay in the warehouse management track, and how should the supervisor course fit into that choice? (by 12 months)

## Ideas, plans, commitments

- Idea: Go freelance as a photographer
- Plan: Take the supervisor course and keep the full-time warehouse lead role while photographing weekends only
- Commitment (confirmed): No commitment confirmed

## Options

| Option | Benefit | Cost | Fits constraints? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Take supervisor course, stay in warehouse lead role, keep photography as a weekend side activity | Maintains current income and benefits; builds leadership credentials; low risk | Limits photography growth; may delay freelance exploration | Yes, fits current constraints |
| Delay or decline course, grow photography freelance on evenings and weekends while keeping warehouse job | Advances creative direction; potential for higher freelance income; flexible | Uncertain income early; less structured career growth; time pressure with evenings and parenting | Uncertain — depends on whether freelance income can meet the no-pay-cut rule and whether evenings are enough |
| Take supervisor course, use new skills in current role, keep photography as side income until it replaces salary | Strongest safety net; skill compounding; possible internal promotion | Still postpones full freelance shift; same time constraints as option 1 | Yes, fits constraints but defers full switch |
